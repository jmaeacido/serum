Dual Action Cleanser

Dual Action Cleanser is a gentle yet effective foaming cleanser designed to remove dirt, oil, and impurities while supporting a refreshed, balanced complexion. Infused with lavender, rose hydrosol, and aloe vera, this botanical-rich formula cleanses without stripping the skin, leaving it feeling soft and hydrated.

Ingredients

Distilled Water, Lavender, Rose Hydrosol, Aloe Vera, Glycerin, Cetearyl Alcohol, E-wax, Medium Chain Triglycerides, Cocamidopropyl Betain, Vitamin E T-50, Xanthan Gum, Germell Plus, CBG Isolate.

Suggested Use

Apply a small amount of cleanser on your face and then use gentle circular motions to lather into the skin. Rinse well with warm water.
