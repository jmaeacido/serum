Retinol Treatment Serum is a powerful yet gentle formula designed to support a smoother, more radiant complexion. Infused with retinol, hyaluronic acid, and vitamin C, this serum helps refine skin tone, reduce the appearance of fine lines, and promote a youthful glow. Nourishing rose hip oil and sweet orange oil provide additional hydration and revitalization.

Ingredients

Distilled Water, Rose Hip Oil, Vitamin C, Polysorbate 80, Cetearyl Alcohol, Vitamin E T-50, Retinol, Hyaluronic Acid, Cannabigerol, Glycerin, Sweet Orange Oil, Optiphan.

Suggested Use

Apply a few drops of serum to your face using the dropper. Gently rub in circular motions into the skin, avoiding the eye area. For optimal results, use at night after cleansing and before moisturizing.
